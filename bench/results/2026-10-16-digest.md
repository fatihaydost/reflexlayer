## Findings

- **Goal (retail, α = 0.05, 30–40 % coverage, held ≥ ~90 %) is not met.** The digest moves retail α = 0.05 from
  0.2 % to 11.4 % coverage at 50/50 (held 50/50, serving in every split) and to 14.0–16.1 % at 70/30 (best: wsr,
  16.1 %, held 50/50).
- Ceiling (diagnostic, full retail data, not a held-out evaluation): digest session-averaged disagreement is 1.2 %
  at threshold 0.95 (18.3 % coverage), 3.2 % at 0.90 (26.0 %), 5.3 % at 0.85 (33.2 %). Even with unlimited
  sessions, α = 0.05 tops out near 26–30 % coverage with this S1; 30–40 % needs a better S1, not a tighter bound.
- α = 0.10: digest + wsr gives 30.5 % (50/50) and 34.4 % (70/30), held 50/50 in both.
- nowrite no longer helps once the digest is in: digest Jev rarely picks a write wrongly at high confidence
  (16 of 95 high-confidence errors), so nowrite mostly removes correct writes (960 of 1,109 blocked rows agreed).
- WSR still never serves at α = 0.05 with ~55 calibration sessions (zero-loss floor ~60), but is the best config
  at 70/30 (~78 sessions).
- Every serving split held, in every view; at this sample size the bounds are conservative rather than tight.

## Caveats

- One dataset, 50 overlapping splits; held counts are not independent trials.
- The digest was written from the policy text, without looking at errors, but after the earlier analysis had
  shown write-vs-respond errors; the confirm-before-write line is in the policy itself.
- 70/30 test halves have 33–34 retail tasks, so its coverage and held numbers are noisier.
- Labels are the teacher's next action on successful runs, not gold.
